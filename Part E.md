## Walkthrough:
### Players
- Player 1 has the Bugatti Chiron Super Sport
- Player 2 has the Tesla Model S Plaid

### Round 1
1. Player 1's turn
2. Player 1 looks at their Bugatti card and chooses Top Speed.
3. Since Player 1 has a higher top speed, they win the round.
4. Player 1 takes both cards.
5. Player 1 chooses the category in the next round.

### Alternative Round
1. If Player 2 had the first turn instead:
2. Player 2 chooses Smart Systems
3. Player 2 wins with a higher smart systems score out of 10.
4. Player 2 takes both cards.
5. Player 2 chooses the category in the next round.

### Example Draw
1. E.g. both players have 9/10 on smart systems.
2. Cards are placed in a central pile
3. The winner of the next round collects all cards in the pile.

### Winning the Game
1. Players start with an equal number of cards.
2. The player whose turn it is selects an attribute.
3. The higher value wins the round.
4. The winner collects all played cards.
5. The winner chooses the next attribute.
6. Play continues until one player has all the cards.
