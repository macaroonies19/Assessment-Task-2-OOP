# Part F — Social, Ethical and Legal Implications
## Individual Impact
The game encourages players to compare cars using measurable attributes such as price, horsepower, and top speed. Players may begin to associate "better" cars with higher values, influencing their perception of what makes a desirable vehicle. For example, a player might believe that a car with a higher top speed is superior, even if they have no practical need for that performance in everyday driving.

The game could encourage bias towards expensive or high performance vehicles because attributes such as price, horsepower, and top speed are often associated with luxury and prestige. Cars with lower prices or lower performance may appear less desirable despite being more practical, affordable, or environmentally friendly.

As the designer, I have a responsibility to present balanced information. This includes:
- Using a variety of attributes rather than focusing solely on performance.
- Including factors such as efficiency, reliability, or environmental impact.
- Clearly explaining how attributes are measured.
- Avoiding language that suggests one type of vehicle is universally superior.

## Social Impact
The game may reinforce social stereotypes by suggesting that expensive vehicles represent success or higher social status. Players may develop the perception that wealthier individuals have access to "better" cars, potentially normalising economic inequalities.

The current system favours:
- Sports cars with high horsepower and top speeds.

It disadvantages:
- Budget cars with potentially lower stats overall.

The design could be made more inclusive by:
- Adding attributes such as safety rating, reliability, seating capacity, and environmental impact.
- Including a wider range of vehicle categories.

## Environmental Impact
The game could unintentionally promote high fuel consumption and powerful vehicles because horsepower and top speed are treated as desirable competitive attributes. This may encourage players to value performance over environmental responsibility.

The current attributes include fuel efficiency, which introduces an environmental factor. However, most of the attributes focus on performance and cost, meaning sustainability receives relatively little attention.

The game could be improved by adding:
- CO₂ emissions (g/km).
- Sustainability score.

## Legal Considerations
Using real world car specifications may create legal issues related to:
- Copyrighted images.
- Trademarked vehicle names and logos.
- Ownership of data collected from commercial websites.
- Accuracy of published specifications.

If information is inspired by websites such as carsales.com.au, I must:
- Verify the accuracy of the data.
- Avoid copying content directly without permission.
- Acknowledge sources where appropriate.
- Ensure the information remains up to date.

To avoid misleading users, I would:
- Clearly state that the game is for entertainment and educational purposes.
- Use verified data from reliable sources.
- Display measurement units consistently.
- Regularly update information when vehicle specifications change.
- Avoid implying that the game provides professional purchasing advice.

## Conclusion
The Car Comparison Top Trumps game provides an engaging way to compare vehicles, but it can influence how players perceive value, status, and performance. While it promotes data analysis and comparison skills, it may also encourage bias toward expensive, powerful cars and overlook environmental considerations. By including a broader range of attributes, promoting sustainability, and ensuring legal and ethical use of data, the game can become fairer, more inclusive, and more socially responsible.
